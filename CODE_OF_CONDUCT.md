# Code of Conduct

This repository is maintained by Ravenhelm LLC. Contributors and reviewers are expected to:

- Be direct, specific, and respectful. Critique the work, not the author.
- Hold the bar set by `docs/_shared/CONTEXT.md` — voice and editorial rules apply to comments and reviews, not just to chapter prose.
- Disclose conflicts of interest (employment with named or implied clients, financial relationships with Linear or competing platforms) when reviewing relevant chapters.
- Keep doctrine debates in the open. Numbered principles change via ADR PRs; private negotiation is out of scope.

Violations may be reported to the repository owner (see `CODEOWNERS`).
